1. Business & messaging use case

RegGuard is a contractor pre-bid diligence product. Users look up a job site and receive Bid Risk Receipts, research summaries, order/PDF-ready notices, and optional Saved Job / ZIP-watch alerts. SMS is transactional / account notification only — not marketing, coupons, or promotional blasts.

2. How end users consent (Call to Action)

  1. User visits https://app.regguardagent.com/ and runs a site lookup.
  2. On Results, they open Text results (SMS).
  3. They enter a US mobile number.
  4. They check an unchecked-by-default consent box (active opt-in).
  5. They tap Text me.

The same consent UI is shown below and at https://app.regguardagent.com/sms-opt-in with no login required.
