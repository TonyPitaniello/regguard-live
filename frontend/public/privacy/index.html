Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026 · Brand: Pitaniello Perkins LLC (RegGuard) · Canonical URL: https://app.regguardagent.com/privacy

RegGuard ("we," "us," or "our"), operated by Pitaniello Perkins LLC, provides construction permitting and site-research tools at app.regguardagent.com. This is the single Privacy Policy that applies to our website, website opt-in flows, and SMS / text messaging program. No other privacy policy applies to RegGuard SMS.

Mobile information & messaging consent (required disclosure)

We do not share, sell, or provide your mobile phone number or messaging consent data to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you submit (name, email address, mobile phone number)
  • Project details (address, trade/project type, and related research inputs)
  • Account and billing information needed to process paid orders
  • Usage data such as pages visited, device/browser type, and basic analytics
  • Communications you send us (support requests, feedback)
  • Messaging consent records when you enter a mobile number in the RegGuard app and request SMS delivery (for example, by tapping "Text me")

How we use information

  • To run research, deliver reports, and operate the RegGuard service
  • To send transactional email or SMS you request (research results, Bid Risk Receipt / share links, order or PDF-ready notices, ZIP-watch alerts you enable, and related account status)
  • To process payments, prevent fraud, and provide customer support
  • To improve product reliability, security, and user experience
  • To comply with legal obligations

We use your mobile phone number and messaging consent only to send the transactional texts described in the SMS program section below, to honor STOP/HELP requests, and to keep records of your consent. We do not use mobile opt-in data for third-party marketing.

SMS / text messaging program

If you provide a mobile number in the RegGuard web app, check the unchecked-by-default consent box, and request SMS (for example, by entering your number and clicking "Text me" after a site lookup — or via the public evidence page at https://app.regguardagent.com/sms-opt-in), you consent to receive transactional text messages from RegGuard / Pitaniello Perkins LLC about that research request, Bid Risk Receipt or shareable report links, related order or PDF-ready notices, and (if you enable them) Saved Job / ZIP-watch alerts.

  • Message frequency varies based on how often you request SMS delivery in the app and whether you enable optional watch alerts.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to opt out of SMS at any time. Reply HELP for help.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
  • Consent to receive SMS is not a condition of purchasing RegGuard services.

We do not share, sell, or provide your mobile phone number or messaging consent data to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

We may use operational service providers that help us deliver messages you requested (for example, our SMS gateway such as Twilio). Those providers process your number only to send the messages you asked for and are not permitted to use your mobile information or messaging consent for their own marketing or promotional purposes.

How we share information (non-SMS vs SMS)

For general account and product operation (not for marketing your mobile number), we may share limited personal information with service providers that help us run RegGuard (hosting, email delivery, payment processing, analytics), with professional advisors or authorities when required by law, or with successors in a merger or acquisition. We do not sell personal information. We do not share, sell, or provide your mobile phone number or messaging consent data to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
